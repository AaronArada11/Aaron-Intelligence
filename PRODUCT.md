# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Portfolio visitors, recruiters, collaborators, classmates, and developers who want a quick sense of Aaron Arada's work, skills, projects, and contact paths.

## Product Purpose

Aaron Intelligence is an AI-powered personal portfolio and chatbot that helps visitors learn about Aaron's projects, education, skills, experience, and achievements without digging through a static resume.

## Brand Personality

Technical, approachable, curious. The experience should feel like a personal developer workspace: direct, interactive, and useful.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing-page polish, decorative UI that distracts from the work, and links or calls to action that feel detached from the terminal-inspired portfolio style.

## Design Principles

- Keep the portfolio interactive and immediately useful.
- Prioritize clear navigation to projects, contact paths, resume, and live demos.
- Preserve the existing theme system and monospace identity.
- Make small additions feel native to the current interface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for readable contrast, keyboard-accessible links and controls, visible focus states, descriptive link text, and motion that does not block access to content.
